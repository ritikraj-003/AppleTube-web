Audio routing is managed by your device
When Bluetooth headphones, earbuds, or speakers are connected to your Android device, audio routes to them automatically.

To switch audio route on Android:

  1. Swipe down from the top of your screen to open Quick Settings.
  2. Tap Media Output or the audio switcher tile.
  3. Select your connected Bluetooth headphones, earbuds, or speaker.
Install on Android Phone
Scan with your phone's camera
Or open this link in Chrome on your phone:
http://localhost:3000
How to install on Android:
1. Open the link above in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
2. Tap "Install App" banner at the top, or tap the 3 dots (⋮) and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3. AppleTube installs as an app on your home screen and plays YouTube music in the background with your screen locked!
